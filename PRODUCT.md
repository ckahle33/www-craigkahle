## Platform

## Users
Anyone who looks Craig Kahle up: friends, peers, colleagues, and the occasional recruiter or MesaWorks lead. It is a personal homepage, not a hiring funnel or a sales page. Confirmed by Craig: "just a personal homepage".

## Product Purpose
A single-page personal site for Craig Kahle, a software engineer who loves music, events and the outdoors. It says who he is, where he works now, where he has worked, where he studied, and how to reach him.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Astro 5 + Tailwind CSS v4, deployed on Vercel on every push to `main`.
- Single page (`src/pages/index.astro`) with Intro, Work, Education and Socials sections; plus a hidden `/system/*` set of theme-demo pages.
- Must keep: avatar photo (`src/images/avatar.jpg`), Education content, Socials links, and a user-switchable light/dark theme.
- Job copy: sharpen wording only, using facts already on the site plus what Craig supplied for Wiz. No new claims, numbers or scope.
- Current role: Support Systems Engineer at Wiz (acquired by Google), since Nov. 2025. Builds tools and systems for support engineers: Terraform, CircleCI, Python, custom CLIs, GCP Workstations. Location currently "Remote" (unconfirmed).
- MesaWorks (mesaworks.dev, Craig's company, since May 2025) continues alongside the Wiz job.
- Each job item links to the employer's real website.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Craig Kahle. Based in Taos, NM (footer shows local Denver time). Interests: music, events, outdoors.

## Evidence on Hand
Avatar photo, career history in `Work.astro`, education history in `Education.astro`, social links in `Socials.astro`. No project screenshots, testimonials or metrics exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles
- Honest and plain: every claim traceable to something Craig said or the site already stated.
- The person leads, not the template: it should feel like Craig's site and not a theme demo.
- Quick to scan, fine to linger on.
- Light and dark are both first-class.
